Where in the West Valley we move

  • Woodland Hills — single-family homes, hillside houses south of Ventura, and the Warner Center mid-rises and condos.
  • Canoga Park — older single-family neighborhoods and garden apartments; easy residential access for most properties.
  • West Hills — newer construction, larger estates, gated communities.
  • Tarzana — hillside homes and Ventura Blvd condos; similar access dynamics to Encino/Sherman Oaks south of the boulevard.
  • Calabasas border — gated communities (Mountain View Estates, Hidden Hills); we handle gate-access and HOA notifications.
  • Adjacent — Reseda, Winnetka, Chatsworth, Bell Canyon.

What's typical for a West Valley move

  • Larger homes & estates — typical inventory is 4+ bedrooms with garages, gym equipment, and patio sets. 4-person crews common.
  • Gated community access — we file gate-clearance requests in advance. Send the gate code or guard-call procedure when you book.
  • Warner Center condos — COIs and freight-elevator reservations common; we coordinate with property management.
  • Garage and patio inventory is often larger than other Valley areas — patio furniture, BBQs, gym equipment, tools. Include these in your inventory list for an accurate quote.

Frequently asked questions

Do you serve Calabasas and Hidden Hills?

Yes — gated-community moves are routine. Send us the gate or guard-station details when you book.

I have a large estate — do you have crews for that?

Yes. Large estates typically need a 4- or 5-person crew with a 26-foot truck (or two trucks). We assess inventory and quote crew size up front.

What about Warner Center high-rises?

Standard mid- and high-rise process: COI filed in advance, freight elevator booked through building, loading dock or curb spot reserved. We file COIs at no charge.

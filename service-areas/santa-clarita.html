SCV neighborhoods we serve

  • Valencia — Westridge, Tesoro del Valle, Bridgeport, Northpark. Master-planned communities with HOA notification requirements and longer cul-de-sac access.
  • Saugus & Canyon Country — older single-family tracts; standard residential access, two-car driveways.
  • Newhall & Old Town — mix of older homes and newer infill apartments downtown.
  • Stevenson Ranch & Castaic — gated communities; we file gate-access requests in advance.
  • Agua Dulce / Acton — rural-residential properties with long driveways and unpaved approaches; we plan truck access ahead of time.

What's typical for an SCV move

Most Santa Clarita moves are single-family homes with full inventories — three to five bedrooms, garages, patio furniture, gym equipment. Crews of 3–4 are common; 4-hour to 8-hour moves are the norm depending on inventory size.

Drive time is the variable: from our Valley dispatch, Santa Clarita is typically 30–45 minutes one-way, which California's double-drive-time rule turns into 60–90 minutes of billable drive time. That cost is visible on your written quote — no surprises.

Common move types in SCV

  • SCV ↔ SFV — Valencia to Encino, Saugus to Granada Hills. Daily occurrence.
  • SCV ↔ Westside / South Bay — longer drive but standard local move.
  • SCV → out of state — long-distance to Phoenix, Las Vegas, Texas, Pacific Northwest is common.
  • Within SCV — Valencia to Saugus, Canyon Country to Stevenson Ranch. Often half-day moves with a 3-person crew.

Frequently asked questions

Do you charge extra to come to Santa Clarita?

No surcharge — just standard double drive time per California regulation. Your written quote shows drive time explicitly.

My HOA requires a moving notification — what do you need?

Send us the HOA notification form when you book. Most just need our company name, COI info (if required), and the move date — we'll fill it out and return it to you for submission.

Can you handle gated-community access?

Yes. Tell us the gate code or guard-call procedure when you book; we'll be on the approved-visitors list before move day.

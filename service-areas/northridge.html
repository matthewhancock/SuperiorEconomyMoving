Northridge is on our doorstep

Northridge is one of our shortest local trips — minimal drive time, which means lower total cost on California's double-drive-time pricing. We do Northridge moves every week and know the neighborhoods well.

Where in Northridge we move

  • North Northridge — single-family homes north of Devonshire, easy access, two-car driveways.
  • Cal State Northridge area — student apartments and condos near CSUN. Lots of summer moves (semester turnover); book early.
  • Porter Ranch border — newer construction, larger homes, gated streets.
  • Northridge mall corridor — mid-rise apartments along Tampa, Nordhoff, Roscoe; COIs sometimes required.
  • Adjacent communities — Granada Hills, Chatsworth, Mission Hills, North Hills.

What's typical for a Northridge move

  • Single-family homes dominate — typical 3–4 bedrooms with garages and full inventory.
  • CSUN student moves are common in late spring (May–June) and late summer (August). Two-bedroom apartment with a 2-person crew, 3–4 hours total.
  • Earthquake history doesn't affect moves directly, but many older Northridge homes have seismic retrofits that affect attic and garage access — tell us if any closets or basements are restricted.

Frequently asked questions

Are Northridge moves quick to schedule?

Yes — we're nearby, so we can usually offer dates within a week or two. Summer student-turnover dates fill up first.

Do you serve Porter Ranch and Granada Hills too?

Yes — both are part of our standard service area, same pricing model as Northridge.

Can you handle a CSUN dorm or student-apartment move?

Absolutely. Smaller inventory means smaller crew and shorter time — typical student move is 2–3 hours with a 2-person crew. Two-hour minimum still applies.

The Valley is what we know best

We dispatch from the San Fernando Valley, so SFV moves get our shortest drive times — which directly affects your bill (California's double-drive-time rule means less drive time = less cost). Most Valley-to-Valley moves are completed in a single day with a 2- or 3-person crew.

Valley neighborhoods we serve

  • East Valley — Burbank, North Hollywood, Toluca Lake, Studio City. Mix of older homes and newer mid-rise apartments along the Ventura/Hollywood Way corridors.
  • Central Valley — Sherman Oaks, Van Nuys, Valley Glen. Sherman Oaks-specific page →
  • South Valley hillsides — Encino, Tarzana, Hidden Hills. Larger homes with longer carries from the curb.
  • West Valley — Woodland Hills & Canoga Park, West Hills, Bell Canyon. Gated communities and West Valley estates.
  • North Valley — Granada Hills, Mission Hills, Northridge, Chatsworth.
  • Central north corridor — Reseda, Winnetka, Canoga Park. Garden-style apartment complexes and post-war homes.

What's typical for a Valley move

  • Single-family homes — Most Valley homes have driveways, attached garages, and standard residential access. Truck parking is rarely a problem.
  • Garden apartments — Reseda, Van Nuys, North Hollywood have lots of two-story walk-up complexes with open-air carries. We quote stair flights up front.
  • Mid-rise apartments along Ventura Blvd — Studio City, Sherman Oaks, Encino. COIs and freight-elevator reservations common.
  • Hillside homes — Encino Hills, Hollywood Hills, Tarzana. Long driveways and switchback access; we plan crew size and truck size accordingly.

Frequently asked questions

Are Valley moves cheaper than moves out of the basin?

Often, yes. Drive time is the biggest variable cost on a local move; since we're based in the Valley, SFV-to-SFV moves have the shortest drive time, which means lower total cost.

Do you cover Burbank and Glendale?

Yes — Burbank, Glendale, La Cañada, and the eastern Valley are part of our standard service area.

What about moves from the Valley to LA or vice versa?

Daily occurrence — Valley to Westside, Valley to DTLA, Valley to South Bay. Standard local move; drive time adjusts to actual mileage.

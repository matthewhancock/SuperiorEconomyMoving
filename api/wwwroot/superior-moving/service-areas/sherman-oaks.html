Sherman Oaks is three areas in one

The "flats" north of Ventura are typical Valley single-family homes. The hillside south of Ventura is narrow, switchback driveways and long carries. The Ventura Blvd corridor is mid-rise apartments and condos with COI requirements. We handle each differently — quote accordingly.

Where in Sherman Oaks we move

  • The flats (north of Ventura) — single-family homes with two-car driveways. Standard residential access.
  • South of Ventura / hillsides — Coldwater Canyon, Beverly Glen access. Narrow streets, long carries, sometimes shuttle trucks. We assess in advance.
  • Ventura Blvd corridor — mid-rise apartments, condos, retail-mix buildings. COIs and freight-elevator reservations common.
  • Sherman Oaks Galleria area — newer condos and apartments with active building management; COI universal.
  • Adjacent — Studio City, Encino, Van Nuys, Valley Glen.

What's typical for a Sherman Oaks move

  • Hillside homes often need shuttle service — the main truck parks legally on a wider street and a smaller vehicle ferries to the house. Quoted up front.
  • Ventura Blvd buildings require COIs (we file at no charge) and freight-elevator reservations (you book; we coordinate).
  • Street parking on Sherman Oaks side streets is restrictive — we arrive early or use loading-zone signage where allowed.

Frequently asked questions

My building wants a COI — is it included?

Yes. Send us the template; we'll file before move day at no charge.

I live up Coldwater Canyon — can your truck reach my house?

Usually yes for a 24-foot truck; larger 26-foot trucks may need a turnout to make tight switchbacks. Send us your address and we'll check the access ahead of time.

Is parking on Ventura a problem?

It can be. For buildings without a loading dock, we arrive at 8 AM to claim a legal spot or post temporary loading-zone signage if the building has one. Worst case, we shuttle from a side street.

What to do with hazardous & restricted items

Movers (us included) can't legally transport hazardous or restricted materials. These need to be disposed of properly before move day:

  • Paint, varnish, solvents — LA Sanitation hazardous-waste roundups (free for LA residents): LA SAFE Centers.
  • Propane tanks — return to a propane supplier or refilling station for proper handling.
  • Ammunition & firearms — LA City sponsors gun buyback events; check with LAPD for current schedule.
  • Bleach, pool chemicals — household hazardous waste; dispose at LA SAFE Centers, never down the drain.
  • Lighter fluid, gasoline, kerosene — household hazardous waste collection only.
  • Batteries (especially lithium) — Home Depot, Lowes, Best Buy accept batteries for recycling.
  • Electronics (e-waste) — California-licensed e-waste collectors take TVs, computers, phones. Best Buy accepts most consumer electronics.

Garage sale resources

For items that still have value, a garage sale 2–3 weekends before your move can offset your moving cost. Saturdays and Sundays from 10 AM to 4 PM are prime time. Advertise dedicated ads for furniture, appliances, and significant items separately from the general garage-sale ad.

  • Craigslist — LA garage sales
  • YardSaleSearch.com
  • Local Buy Nothing / neighborhood Facebook groups — increasingly the most active venue
  • Nextdoor — neighborhood-specific selling

USPS change of address

USPS Mover's Guide — file at least 2 weeks before move day. Includes a packet of mover-specific coupons and offers.

Need bulky items hauled instead of donated? We don't run a junk-hauling service, but most LA residents qualify for free LA Sanitation bulky-item pickup (link above). For everything else, the donation organizations above are good first calls.
